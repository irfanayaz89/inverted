INVERTED

Five tiny games that fight back. Hold the phone any way you like: it stays upside down. Turn it sideways and it mirrors.

Tilt puzzle
◐Negative Space

Roll a ball by tilting. Flip the phone over and black and white swap, so walls become floor. Flip in the wrong place and you get crushed.
Reflex
↯Wrong Way Up

Swipe the way the word says. Red words mean the opposite, the arrow lies, and "up" is the game's up, not yours.
Arcade
▤Ceiling Stack

Tap to drop sliding blocks and build a tower that hangs from the sky. Tilting blows the block like wind.
Typing test
⌨Upside Type

A 60 second typing test on an upside down keyboard that gets mirrored, then alphabetical, then scrambled.
Brain trainer
✎Mirror Trace

Trace a shape in one stroke, but your line is reflected away from your finger. Six shapes, six ways to be wrong.

Best on a phone. On iPhone, tap Allow when asked for motion access.
On a laptop: keys 1 to 6 fake the phone's pose, arrows fake tilt.
